**WEBSITE DESIGN \& LAUNCH RULES**



The website must feel polished, professional, modern, credible, and intentional. It should never feel like an AI-generated template, a design experiment, or a generic project.



**DESIGN RULES**



* Never use purple gradients.
* Never use excessive gradients, glassmorphism, fake metallic effects, or other gimmicky visual effects.
* Never use cheap-looking or overly decorative UI elements.
* Do not overuse pill-shaped buttons. Buttons should be clean, simple, and purposeful.
* Never use fake reviews, testimonials, customer quotes, ratings, or fabricated social proof.
* Never use fake customer counters, statistics, numbers, or results.
* Do not use oversized hero text or massive headlines.
* Do not use emoji icons.
* Use professional, consistent icons only when they genuinely improve usability.
* Do not overuse em dashes.
* Do not use excessive scroll animations.
* Do not use unnecessary animations or motion effects.
* Never use cursor-following animations or cursor effects.
* Never use AI-generated stock photos or obviously AI-generated imagery.
* Do not add visual effects simply to make the website look impressive.
* Keep the design clean, restrained, balanced, and professional.



**CONTENT \& AUTHENTICITY**



* Everything on the website must be truthful and intentional.
* Never invent reviews, customers, testimonials, statistics, results, partnerships, credentials, or achievements.
* Never create fake social proof.
* Never use placeholder content that could be mistaken for real information.
* Do not add unnecessary sections just to make the website look bigger or more complete.
* Every section, component, image, button, and piece of content must have a clear purpose.



**LAUNCH REQUIREMENTS**



* The website must NOT be considered ready to launch until ALL of the following are completed:
* A custom domain is connected and working correctly.
* A favicon has been added.
* The "Made with AI" tag or badge has been completely removed.
* A dedicated Privacy Policy page has been added.
* A dedicated Terms and Conditions page has been added.
* All navigation links have been tested.
* All buttons have been tested.
* All forms have been tested.
* All interactive elements have been tested.
* The website works properly on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* There are no broken links, missing assets, layout issues, or obvious visual bugs.
* All text has been reviewed for spelling, grammar, consistency, and accuracy.
* The entire website has been reviewed before launch.



**FINAL STANDARD**



* The final website should look like a professionally designed, production-ready website.
* Do not prioritize trends over quality.
* Do not add features just because they are possible.
* Do not fabricate anything.
* Do not ship unfinished work.
* Do not launch until every requirement above has been checked and verified.



**MOST IMPORTANTLY:**



MAKE NO MISTAKES.



REVIEW THE ENTIRE WEBSITE CAREFULLY BEFORE CONSIDERING IT READY TO LAUNCH.
